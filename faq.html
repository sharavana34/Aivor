General

AIVOR is a suite of MetaTrader 5 expert advisors and related tools by Vyuha Labs (Artificial Intelligence for Vision, Optimisation & Research). Each product can have different rules, settings, and report availability. Review the Products page and the product documentation before use.

You should understand basics such as lot size, leverage, spread, equity, and drawdown before running an EA. Start on a demo account and learn how the strategy behaves before risking real money. See How It Works for the recommended workflow.

A browser-only utility on the Analyze page that reads MetaTrader 5 Trade History or Strategy Tester Excel exports on your device. Files stay on your computer; nothing is uploaded to a server.

Products & EAs

They are different trading styles on MetaTrader 5 (primarily XAUUSD). HF targets more frequent opportunities and currently has both live and backtest reports. Scalper targets shorter-term setups and Swing longer-hold trades; available reports for Scalper and Swing are backtests only. Names alone do not guarantee a holding time or outcome. Compare details on Products and report types on Performance.

Check each product’s supported symbols and your broker’s symbol naming (suffixes can differ). Verify compatibility before attaching an EA to a chart. Current product specs are listed on Products.

Only change parameters the specific EA exposes and that you understand. Review the product manual and test changes on demo first. Increasing lot size can increase losses as well as potential gains.

MT5 Setup

Follow the installation steps supplied with the product. Typically you open MT5’s data folder, place the EA in the Experts folder, refresh or restart MT5, then attach it to a compatible chart. If the product instructions differ, follow those. Overview of the workflow: How It Works.

Copy Trading

No. With an EA, the software runs on your MT5 terminal or VPS. Copy trading generally mirrors trades through a broker or provider’s copy-trading service. Requirements, fees, controls, and risks can differ. See the Copy Trading directory for current setups.

Check the Copy Trading page for the latest verified broker-specific setups and destination links. A broker name appearing elsewhere does not by itself mean a strategy is configured or available on that broker.

Performance & Reports

Live results come from actual trading activity; backtests simulate a strategy against historical data and assumptions. Backtests may not reflect real spreads, slippage, liquidity, or future conditions. On this site, HF currently has live and backtest reports; Scalper and Swing have backtest reports only. Always check which type you are viewing on Performance. Do not treat backtests as live results.

A report may not yet be supplied, imported, or mapped to the selected product and report type. The site shows an unavailable status rather than inventing metrics. Check Performance again when the relevant report has been published.

Risk & Safety

No. Trading involves substantial risk of loss, and you can lose some or all of your capital. Past performance and backtest results do not guarantee future outcomes. AIVOR systems are tools—not financial advice.

Drawdown measures a decline from an account’s equity or balance peak to a later low, depending on how the metric is defined. Always check whether a report uses balance-based or equity-based drawdown and whether the figure is absolute or percentage-based.

Verify product and broker compatibility, test on demo, understand the drawdown you could tolerate, use conservative position sizing, check spreads and commissions, and monitor the account. Never trade money you cannot afford to lose. More context: How It Works.
