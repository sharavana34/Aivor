Risk & readiness

Know the risks before you start.

Automated trading does not remove market risk. Use these checks before connecting a system to a live account.

◆

Understand your exposure

Profitability is not guaranteed. Market gaps, volatility, spread widening, slippage, execution delays, and technical failures can affect results.

  • Start on demo and validate the settings.
  • Use a lot size you can afford to risk.
  • Know the maximum drawdown and stop conditions.
⌁

Check the setup

Broker symbols and execution conditions can differ. A setup that works in one environment may behave differently elsewhere.

  • Confirm symbol suffixes and trading hours.
  • Review commission, spread, and leverage.
  • Monitor the terminal and VPS connection.
