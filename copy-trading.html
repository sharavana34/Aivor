Copy trading

Follow the live Aivor account instead of installing the Expert Advisor yourself. Your broker copies the provider’s trades into your account, in proportion to the capital you allocate.

How it works

You open an account with the listed broker, connect it to the copy service, and choose how much to allocate. When Aivor opens or closes a gold trade, the service mirrors that action on your side. You can stop copying at any time.
